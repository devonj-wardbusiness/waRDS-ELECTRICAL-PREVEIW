Common dedicated circuit jobs

If it draws significant power, it probably needs its own circuit.

Garage & Workshop

Welder, compressor, table saw, dust collector — we size the circuit to the tool's actual draw and future-proof with enough capacity for upgrades.

Kitchen Appliances

Microwave, disposal, refrigerator, dishwasher — code requires dedicated circuits for most of these. We do it clean and labeled.

HVAC & Mini-Splits

Heat pumps, mini-splits, electric water heaters, and baseboard heaters all need properly sized dedicated circuits.

Hot Tubs & Outdoor

Hot tubs require a dedicated 240V GFCI circuit with a disconnect box. Site-dependent — we assess the run and conditions before quoting.

Signs you need a dedicated circuit

✅ An appliance trips the breaker when running with other loads
✅ Lights dim when a large appliance starts up
✅ You're installing a new appliance with a specific amperage requirement
✅ Adding a workshop, garage, or outdoor space with power tools
✅ Your panel has no spare breaker slots (may need a panel upgrade too)
✅ Inspector flagged a shared circuit during a home sale
