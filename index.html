Common Questions

Good questions.

Do you pull permits?
Yes — any work that requires a permit gets one. We handle the application, schedule the inspection, and make sure it closes. Work without a permit can void your homeowner's insurance and cause problems when you sell.
Do you service my area?
We serve Colorado Springs, Falcon/Peyton, Woodland Park, and Pueblo. If you're nearby and not sure, call or text — we'll tell you straight.
How do flat-rate quotes work?
After we see the job (in person or with enough detail), we give you one price for the complete work. That price doesn't change unless the scope does — and if it does, we tell you before proceeding. No hourly surprises.
Can you do emergency or same-day work?
Yes. If you have a hazard — burning smell, sparking, outage — call us directly at 915-487-0660. For same-day non-emergency jobs, fill out the form or text us. We keep slots open for urgent calls most days.
What's the $65 service call fee?
The $65 diagnostic fee covers our time to come out and assess the problem. If we fix it the same visit, that $65 applies toward the labor — it's not an extra charge on top.
