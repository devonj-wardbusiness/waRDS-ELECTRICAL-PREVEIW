Common Questions

Do you pull permits?
Yes — any work that requires a permit in Colorado Springs or surrounding areas gets one. We handle the application, schedule the inspection, and make sure it closes. Work without a permit can void insurance and cause issues when you sell your home.
Do you service my area?
We serve Colorado Springs, Falcon/Peyton, Woodland Park, and Pueblo. If you're nearby and not sure, call or text — we'll tell you straight.
How do flat-rate quotes work?
After we see the job (in person or by enough detail), we give you one price for the complete work. That price doesn't change unless the scope does — and if it does, we tell you before proceeding. No hourly surprises.
Can you do emergency or same-day work?
Yes. If you have a hazard — burning smell, sparking, outage — call us directly at 915-487-0660. For same-day non-emergency jobs, book online or text and we'll confirm availability. We keep slots open for urgent calls most days.
What panel brands and equipment do you work with?
We install and service Square D, Eaton, Siemens, Leviton, and other major brands. We don't touch Federal Pacific or Zinsco panels for repair — those need replacement, and we'll tell you why. For EV chargers, we work with any EVSE brand.
