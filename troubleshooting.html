What we troubleshoot

We diagnose the actual cause — not just reset a breaker and leave.

Tripping Breakers

Overloaded circuits, faulty breakers, wiring issues, or nuisance trips from aging AFCI/GFCI devices — we find the real cause.

Dead Outlets & Switches

Wiring failures, tripped GFCI upstream, loose connections, or damage. We trace it back to the source.

Flickering Lights

Loose neutrals, bad connections, overloaded circuits, or failing fixtures. Flickering is often a sign of a bigger problem.

Burning Smells / Hot Outlets

This is a potential fire hazard. We prioritize these calls — shut the circuit off and call us immediately.

When to call right away

🚨 Burning smell from outlets, panel, or walls
🚨 Sparking when plugging in devices
🚨 Breaker that won't reset or keeps tripping immediately
🚨 Outlets or switches that feel warm or hot
🚨 Power out in part of the house with no clear cause
🚨 Any shock when touching outlets or appliances
