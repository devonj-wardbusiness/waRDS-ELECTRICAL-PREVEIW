Common safety & code corrections

We address these frequently — often as part of a home sale or post-inspection punch list.

GFCI Corrections

Bathrooms, kitchens, garages, outdoors, crawlspaces — GFCI protection is required in all wet/damp locations. We install and test each one properly.

AFCI Requirements

Many bedrooms and living areas now require AFCI protection. We can add AFCI breakers or combination devices where needed.

Grounding & Bonding

Missing ground wires, improper bonding on metal piping, or no ground rod — we identify and correct the specific issue without guessing.

Smoke & CO Detectors

Hardwired smoke/CO detector installs with battery backup — interconnected so one alert trips all of them.

Service Hazard Repairs

Double-tapped breakers, open junction boxes, exposed wire, undersized conductors, aluminum branch wiring — we fix it right.

Panel Labeling

Every breaker identified, labeled clearly, and mapped to the actual circuit. Useful for inspection and for you long-term.
