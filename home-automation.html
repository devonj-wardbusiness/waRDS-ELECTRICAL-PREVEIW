Common questions about home automation

Answers to what Colorado Springs homeowners ask most.

Can one switch really control all the lights in my house?
Yes. With a centralized control panel or smart hub, we wire your lighting circuits so a single switch, keypad, or app can trigger a "scene" that turns off every light in the house — including the garage, basement, and outdoor fixtures you normally forget about.
Do I need to rewire my whole house for home automation?
Usually not. Most smart switches and dimmers install in your existing switch boxes as long as there's a neutral wire present, which we verify during the walkthrough. For whole-home scenes and centralized panels, we may add a few low-voltage control wires, but it's rarely a full rewire.
What is a lighting "scene" and how does it work?
A scene is a saved combination of lights, brightness levels, and on/off states that activates with one tap — for example, an "Away" scene that shuts off every light and locks down the house, or a "Movie Night" scene that dims the living room and turns off the kitchen.
Will smart switches work with my existing light fixtures?
Most LED and incandescent fixtures work fine with smart switches and dimmers — we just make sure the dimmer is rated for your bulb type and load. We'll check compatibility during the quote and recommend the right hardware for each room.
