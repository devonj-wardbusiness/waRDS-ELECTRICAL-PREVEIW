What's included

A complete EV charger install — not just plugging in a unit.

Panel Load Check

We calculate your existing load before sizing the new circuit. If your panel is at capacity, we tell you upfront — no surprises on install day.

Correct Breaker Sizing

Most Level 2 chargers need a 50A circuit (40A continuous). We size it to match your charger and future-proof for faster charging upgrades.

Clean Conduit Run

Wire routed through conduit where exposed — clean, protected, and inspectable. Not just stapled through walls and hoped for the best.

Permit Coordination

Most jurisdictions require a permit for new circuits. We handle the coordination and know what inspectors look for so your install closes clean.

Signs you're ready for a Level 2 charger

✅ You're tired of slow L1 charging overnight
✅ You drive enough daily miles to need full-charge overnight
✅ You have a dedicated parking spot with garage access
✅ You're adding a second EV and need two circuits
✅ You want to take advantage of off-peak charging rates
✅ Your EV supports 11kW or 19kW charging speeds
